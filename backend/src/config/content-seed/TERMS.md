The acceptable use policy for OrkaVault: who may use it, how shared and personal credentials may be used, and what is monitored.

## Who may use OrkaVault {#who-may-use}

OrkaVault is PROJXON's internal system for governing access to shared credentials. Only current PROJXON members whose registration has been approved by an administrator may use it. Access is personal: do not share your sign-in, your multi-factor device, or an active session with anyone, including other PROJXON members.

Using OrkaVault means you agree to these terms and to the [Privacy notice](/legal/privacy). If a newer version of these terms is published you will be asked to accept it the next time you sign in.

## Shared credentials {#shared-credentials}

Credentials in the shared vault belong to PROJXON. When you are granted access to one:

- Use it only for the work purpose you gave in your access request, and only while the grant is active.
- Request the shortest access type that does the job (90-second view, 24 hours, or ongoing).
- Do not copy a credential out of OrkaVault into other password managers, documents, chat messages, email, tickets, or source code, and do not screenshot or print it.
- Do not pass a credential, one-time code, or QR code on to anyone else. They must request their own access.
- Do not try to access a credential you were not granted, or to get around clearance, department, or approval rules.
- Report a credential you think is exposed, or access you should not have, to an administrator straight away.

Managers and administrators who approve requests are responsible for granting only the access a request actually needs, and for revoking access that is no longer needed.

## Personal Vault {#personal-vault}

> The Personal Vault is being rolled out. This section applies once it is available to you.

The Personal Vault is a private space for your own credentials. You may store personal and work credentials in it, as long as storing them there does not break another PROJXON policy. Shared PROJXON credentials belong in the shared vault, not in your Personal Vault.

Your Personal Vault is encrypted in your browser with a vault passphrase that only you know, and protected by a one-time recovery key shown to you once at setup. You are responsible for keeping both safe. **If you lose both your vault passphrase and your recovery key, PROJXON cannot recover your Personal Vault data.** No administrator can reset it for you, other than by wiping it.

## Monitoring and logging {#monitoring}

OrkaVault is a monitored system. Activity is recorded to protect PROJXON's credentials and to investigate misuse, consistent with the System Use Notice shown at sign-in. Recorded activity includes:

- Access requests, approvals, denials, grant changes and revocations, with the reason you gave.
- Each time a shared password, one-time code, or QR code is revealed.
- Changes to shared credentials and administrative changes to user accounts.
- The IP address the action came from and when it happened.

Where PROJXON's Google Workspace monitoring is enabled, administrators can also see Google Workspace sign-in events, connected third-party apps, and registered devices for your PROJXON Google account. See the [Privacy notice](/legal/privacy#data-we-collect) for details.

The contents of your Personal Vault are never visible to PROJXON. Only the fact that it exists and when it changed are known to the server.

## Your account and sign-in {#your-account}

- Multi-factor authentication is required. Keep your authenticator app and any remembered devices secure.
- Sign out on shared or public computers, and do not choose "Remember this device" on them.
- Tell an administrator straight away if you lose a device or think your account has been compromised.

## Leaving PROJXON {#offboarding}

When your engagement with PROJXON ends, or when an administrator deactivates your account, your OrkaVault account is deactivated and all of your access grants to shared credentials end. Records of past activity are kept as described in the Privacy notice.
